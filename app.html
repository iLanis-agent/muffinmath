Muffin Math

Boundary: illustrative arithmetic only Labeled kitchen norms - half a cup of sugar per cup of flour is the cupcake line, 10 to 12 strokes is just-combined, a third of a cup fills a standard well. Your recipe and your tin win every tie. This page never judges doneness or food safety; follow your recipe's bake guidance.

The batter truth

Past half a cup of sugar per cup of flour, a muffin is a cupcake.

The stir truth

Muffin batter wants 10 to 12 strokes, just until combined.

The scoop truth

A standard muffin well takes a third of a cup of batter.
